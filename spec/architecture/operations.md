# What is still done by hand

Every act an operator performs on the platform's own services and nodes, and on infra beneath them:
how it is done today, how often, and the capability and RFC that take it over. RFC bz8nz makes this
the measure of the platform running itself: it is self-hosted when every row is `Done` or `Boot`,
and no app is brought onto it before then. A row closes in the commit that makes it `Done`; an act
with no RFC is given one before it is worked on. Taken on 2026-10-14 from both repositories' specs
and tasks, the RFCs and the last 300 commits of each.

## How a row is classed

- **Done** -- the platform does it with no person: CI, host, keeper, the deployer, cron, the relay,
  or a write on the control contract (`mise run platform ...` or the console, RFC cpc2b).
- **Task** -- a `mise run` task on the author's machine that reaches a node by SSH or a forward over
  it, or uses a credential decrypted from sops on the laptop. Counted as hand: bz8nz forbids SSH,
  sops by hand and laptop credentials whether or not a script wraps them.
- **Hand** -- an edit to git that is really configuration, a provider dashboard, `gh`, `wrangler`,
  GitHub's UI, or a shell on the node.
- **Boot** -- marked where the act is a candidate for bz8nz's bootstrap exception (what must exist
  before the registry does).

RFC status is the RFC's own field; "partly built" is read off the commits and the specs that cite it.
An RFC id in **bold** under "Replaced by" means none exists: the act has **no RFC**.

## Acts with no RFC

Each needs one before it is worked on, per bz8nz:

- **Nodes**: N2 a machine's base (OS, Docker, Tailscale login, btrfs `/data`); N7 restarting dockerd
  after `daemon.json`; N19 Tailscale naming. N1 is the provider's and likely stays bootstrap.
- **Deploy**: D3 a rolling upgrade of a manual (stateful) app as a workflow -- the most frequent
  hand act of the week; D9 dispatching a run; D12 a Postgres major.
- **Config**: C4 a node's role grants held in the registry.
- **Secrets**: S6 Cloudflare token rotation.
- **Data**: DA3 the two-cores-lost failover as a guarded action; DA7 restore and a scheduled restore
  drill.
- **Network and DNS**: NW1 DNS records; NW2 Access applications; NW3 who runs `rules sync` (CI or a
  controller, not the laptop); NW6 Email Routing. Together these are "the edge as resources".
- **Workers**: W4 the deployer handing a Worker its secrets from the secret service; W5 Worker
  rollback; W6 custom domains of the non-gateway Workers.
- **CI**: CI2 GitHub Actions secrets and variables.
- **Observability**: O3 notifications (the roadmap names it, no RFC); O5 logs read off the node.

## Proposed order

Ordered by hand acts removed, each step's dependencies already met by the steps before it or by what
is built.

1. **host writes each app's environment from the secret service** (231yq's unbuilt half, then its
   tier 1 import). The service, the node tokens' digests and the `config`/`secret` classes are built;
   only host's read is missing. Removes or collapses C1, C2, S1 for tier 1, S2, S3, W8, and the
   `secret.env` writes inside DA1, S5, S7 and `registry token`. It is also what 6jw04 (DA1), 024i3
   (S4) and the deployer handing Workers secrets (W4) wait on. **About 10 rows.**
2. **host takes authority from the registry past the canary** (jln34: shadow, canary, then node by
   node), with restart, redeploy and rollback as `app` actions on the contract (cpc2b, 2p4a1). The
   shadow and the canary path are built. Removes D4, D5, D6, D7, D8, N13, N18, and ends the import
   resetting placements (N4). **About 8 rows.**
3. **Everything that lists the nodes reads the registry, and one VPC Network binding** (qcyb3):
   host's `REGISTRY`, the egress proxies, the database proxy's members, `WORKER_RESOURCES` admitting
   one binding, then `nodes.toml` retired as a source (ja5hm's import-then-remove). Needs 2. Removes
   N3, N4, N9, N10, N14, N15, C4 if grants move with it. **About 7 rows**, and adding a node drops
   from about a dozen acts to N1, N2, N6, N8, N11, N12.
4. **The Workers onto the deployer, with bindings and secrets from the platform** (4s460, ja5hm's
   `[worker]` bindings, W4's missing RFC, W5's missing RFC). Needs 1 for secrets and 3 for bindings.
   Removes W2, W4, W5, W7, W9, and N9's remainder. **About 5 rows.**
5. **Stateful rollouts and the database's acts as ledger workflows** (23wgz, whose ledger runs and
   executor landed on 2026-10-13/14; an RFC for D3; 6jw04; dk6wz's database acts). Needs 1. Removes
   D3, DA1, DA2, DA4, and gives DA7 a home. **About 5 rows**, D3 alone being the most frequent hand
   act of 2026-10-13 and 14.
6. **A node brought up as a workflow from the console** (dk6wz's first act; a tunnel controller for
   N8; wkv8p for the core; 8vy7x's key for identity). Needs 3 and 5. Leaves only N1, N2 and the
   first host as bootstrap; removes N6's reruns, N8, N12, N16, N17. **About 5 rows.**
7. **The edge as resources** -- DNS records, Access applications, custom domains, WAF sync from a
   controller rather than the laptop (all **without RFCs**: NW1, NW2, NW3, W6, NW6; with 8qh17 for
   the whitelists). Independent of 1-6 and could run beside them. **About 6 rows.**
8. **Sources and GitHub in the registry** (eiazr, 024i3, rpkku; CI2 needs an RFC). Needs 1. Removes
   C3, CI2, CI3, S4. **About 4 rows.**
9. **Stores and buckets as resources** (vh1bl), their keys in the secret service. Needs 1 and cpc2b.
   Removes DA8, S5. **About 2 rows.**
10. **Being told, not looking** -- notifications (needs an RFC), np65b, yu4kw, beos3, logs (O5 needs
    an RFC). Independent; low row count but it is what makes the rest safe to leave unwatched.
    **About 5 rows.**

After 1-6 the hand acts left are bootstrap (N1, N2, N11, S8, S9, W3), the edge (step 7), GitHub
(step 8) and observability (step 10). Steps 1 and 2 are the two whose prerequisites are already
running, and between them they cover the acts done on 2026-10-12 to 14 most often.
